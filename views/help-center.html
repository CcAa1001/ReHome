Getting Started

Simply tap "Sign Up" on the homepage and enter your email address and a password. You can also sign up using your Google or Apple account for faster registration. Once verified, you're ready to browse and buy!
Yes! Creating an account, browsing, and buying items on ReHome is completely free. Sellers pay a small 8% commission fee on completed sales to help maintain the platform.
ReHome focuses exclusively on premium secondhand furniture and home goods with a commitment to sustainability. We verify seller quality, use eco-friendly packaging, and plant a tree for every 10 items sold on our platform.

Buying

Every listing includes detailed photos and a condition rating (New, Like New, Good, Fair). Sellers are required to disclose any defects. You can also message the seller directly to ask questions before purchasing.
Yes! Many listings have a "Make Offer" button. Sellers can accept, decline, or counter your offer. If accepted, the transaction proceeds at the agreed price.
Absolutely. All purchases are covered by ReHome Buyer Protection. If an item arrives damaged, doesn't match the description, or never arrives, you're eligible for a full refund. Payment is held in escrow until you confirm satisfaction.
Tap the heart icon on any listing to add it to your wishlist. You can view and manage your saved items from the "Favorites" section of your profile. You'll also get notified if a saved item's price drops.

Selling

Tap the "Sell" button, upload at least 3 photos of your item, add a title and description, set your price, and choose a category. Your listing will be live within minutes after a quick review.
ReHome charges an 8% commission on completed sales. There are no listing fees or monthly subscriptions. You only pay when your item successfully sells.
Payment is released to your account 3 days after the buyer confirms receipt of the item (or automatically after 14 days if no issues are reported). You can withdraw funds to your bank account or PayPal.

Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are encrypted and processed through our secure payment partner.
Yes. We use PCI DSS compliant payment processing with 256-bit SSL encryption. Your card details are never stored on our servers — they're handled directly by our payment partner, Stripe.
Yes! For purchases over $200, you can choose to pay in 4 interest-free installments through our partner Klarna. Just select "Pay in 4" at checkout.

Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free for orders over $100. Express shipping (2-3 days) is available for $15. Local pickup is also free where available.
Yes! Once your item ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track all your orders from the "My Orders" section of your account dashboard.
Yes! For larger items, we offer white-glove delivery service which includes in-home placement and packaging removal. This premium service is available for an additional fee based on your location.

Account

Tap "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and we'll send you a reset link. The link expires after 24 hours for security reasons.
