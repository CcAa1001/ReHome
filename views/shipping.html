Eco-Friendly Packaging

Sustainability doesn't stop at the furniture. Every shipment from ReHome is packaged with the environment in mind.

Recycled Materials

All our packaging is made from 100% post-consumer recycled cardboard and paper products.

Biodegradable Peanuts

We use starch-based packing peanuts that dissolve in water — no styrofoam, ever.

Reusable Boxes

Our sturdy shipping boxes are designed to be reused. Pass them on or recycle when done.

Order Tracking

Real-Time Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. Use it to follow your package from our warehouse to your doorstep. You can also track your order anytime from your account dashboard under "My Orders."
