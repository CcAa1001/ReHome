How It Works

1

Create Your Profile

Set up your shop profile with a clear avatar, a short bio, and your location. Buyers love purchasing from sellers they can trust and relate to. A complete profile increases sales by up to 40%.

2

List Your Items

Use our intuitive listing form. Upload multiple high-quality photos, write a detailed description including dimensions and flaws, and specify the brand or maker if known.

3

Set a Fair Price

We recommend pricing items at 40-60% of their original retail value depending on the condition. You can also accept offers to negotiate directly with interested buyers.

4

Ship With Care

Once sold, pack your item securely using eco-friendly materials whenever possible. We provide shipping labels for smaller items, while large furniture can be arranged for local pickup or professional freight.

Pro Seller Tips

Photography

  • Use natural daylight when possible
  • Clean the item before photographing
  • Take photos from multiple angles (front, side, back)
  • Include close-ups of textures, labels, and any flaws
  • Use a clean, uncluttered background

Descriptions

  • Be honest about the condition (scratches, stains)
  • Include precise dimensions (W x D x H)
  • Mention the material (e.g., solid oak, top-grain leather)
  • Share the history of the item if it's vintage
  • Use relevant keywords so buyers can find it easily
