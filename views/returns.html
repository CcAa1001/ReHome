30-Day Return Window

Changed your mind? No problem. You have 30 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return or exchange for any item purchased on ReHome. Items must meet our return conditions outlined below.

How to Return an Item

Our return process is designed to be as smooth as possible. Just follow these three steps.

1

Start Your Request

Log in to your account, go to "My Orders," and select the item you'd like to return. Fill out the reason for return.

2

Ship It Back

We'll email you a prepaid shipping label. Pack the item securely in its original packaging and drop it off at any carrier location.

3

Get Your Refund

Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be processed. You'll receive confirmation via email.

Return Conditions

To qualify for a return, items must meet the following criteria:

Item must be in its original, unused condition with no signs of wear or damage
Original packaging, tags, and labels must be intact and included
Return request must be initiated within 30 days of delivery date
Proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt) must be provided
Items must be returned via the prepaid label provided by ReHome

Refund Timeline

After we receive and inspect your returned item, refunds are processed as follows:

Original Credit/Debit Card 5–10 business days
PayPal 3–5 business days
ReHome Store Credit Instant
Bank Transfer 7–14 business days

Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom-made or personalized furniture pieces
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "As-Is"
  • Assembled flat-pack furniture (if assembly instructions were followed)
  • Items with hygiene seals that have been broken (mattresses, cushions)
  • Gift cards and store credit vouchers
